# Pilot Animation Build Plan

## Story beats and required animation

1. **Evening discomfort:** Maya fans herself; animated heat shimmer above road.
2. **Thermal storage:** Sunlight rays strike road and roofs; surfaces warm; night transition; heat waves continue.
3. **Vegetation:** Split-screen tree shade and leaf-water vapour versus bare paved surface.
4. **Street-level variation:** Park, street canyon and car park; camera pan; no numerical temperature labels.
5. **Waste heat:** Car, building and outdoor AC icons; heat arrows; urban/rural comparison.
6. **Interventions:** Trees grow in, roof becomes reflective, green roof appears; heat waves reduce.
7. **Close:** Maya and Leo deliver final line; end card.

## Character continuity

- Maya: curious presenter; blue accent; expressive head tilt and arm gestures.
- Leo: explanatory presenter; green accent; points to diagrams and objects.
- Keep head/body/limb proportions and colour accents consistent between shots.
- Expressions should be communicated with simple eyes, brows and mouth shapes, not only captions.

## Rendering and sound acceptance

- Final pilot: 1920×1080, 24 fps, 16:9.
- Narration: British English male voice; test pronunciation and pacing before full render.
- Music: quiet, licensed or explicitly public-domain track; duck under narration.
- Sound effects: only from sources with clear reuse rights.
- Subtitles: timed SRT checked against final narration.
- QC: watch the entire MP4; check audio sync, text legibility, clipping, frame rate, resolution and source credits.

## Build status

This is a production plan, not a completed render. The first Manim smoke-test scene exists in the repository; the full pilot still needs implementation and a successful render.
