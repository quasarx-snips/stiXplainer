# Pilot 01 — Why do cities feel hotter than the countryside?

**Format:** 60–90 second animated explainer  
**Status:** Story outline only; narration, sourced facts, visual assets and final render are pending.

## Editorial promise

Explain the urban heat-island effect in plain language without implying that every city or neighbourhood behaves identically.

## Beat sheet

| Approx. time | Narration beat | Visual direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | A hot day can feel even hotter in a city. | Two stick-figure presenters stand in a city street; heat shimmer animates above the road. |
| 8–22s | Dark roofs and asphalt absorb and store solar energy. | A simple sun-to-road illustration; surface changes from bright to dark, then releases heat waves. |
| 22–38s | Trees cool their surroundings through shade and evapotranspiration. | A bare pavement scene transitions to a tree; shade spreads and animated water vapour rises from leaves. |
| 38–55s | Buildings, traffic and waste heat can add to the effect. | Moving traffic and building silhouettes; arrows show heat accumulating between buildings. |
| 55–75s | The effect varies by place, weather, materials and time of day. | Split-screen neighbourhood comparison; presenters summarize the key idea. |
| 75–90s | Small design choices can help make neighbourhoods cooler. | Tree canopy, reflective roof and shaded walking route; closing title. |

## Fact-check requirements

Before recording a factual script:
- Use authoritative sources such as national meteorological agencies, universities and peer-reviewed urban-climate research.
- Distinguish surface temperature from air temperature.
- Avoid a universal numerical temperature claim unless its location, method and source are stated.
- Add source links and publication details to `sources.md`.

## Production requirements

The finished pilot should include two animated characters, expression/pose changes, moving explanatory illustrations, one or two rights-cleared photo inserts, British English male narration, licensed music and effects, synchronized subtitles and a 1080p/24 fps master.
